Act one · Who we are

Theatre is more than performance.

It is confidence. It is imagination. It is self-discovery.

The CHIREC Drama Collective is a group of young performers from the #1 international school in Telangana who come together to learn, experiment, create and put on a show.

These students are not only putting on a show for you, they are building skills that will stay with them long after the curtain closes, including communication, collaboration, discipline, empathy, leadership, and creative problem solving.

What we are building

A sustainable platform where young performers can learn, collaborate and experiment through productions, workshops and future theatre initiatives. With the support of partners like you, we hope to make theatre more accessible to young people and encourage a new generation to discover, enjoy, and pursue the performing arts.

Act two · Productions

Written, directed, designed, performed and managed by students.

October 2026

The annual full-scale production

Once a year, we put up a full-scale play. It is one of our outputs, not the culmination of everything we do.

Venue
CHIREC International School – ISRP Campus
Dates
Between 22 and 29 October 2026

  1. Bi-weekly workshops

    Led by experts and senior students: practical skills (improvisation, method acting, miming, staging) and some theory (theatre history, acting techniques). The goal is for every member to actually learn something, not just participate.

  2. Drama publication

    Our own drama-specific publication: articles on theatre history, reflections from members, role descriptions, interviews, updates on the collective, and original written or creative work by students.

  3. Community outreach

    Plans with student-led initiatives including Chirec Radio, and Project SignVerse, an initiative by a Chirec student focused on inclusive communication with the deaf and hard-of-hearing community: teaching mute theatre and mime, performing mute plays, and helping them organise their own.

  4. Awareness through performance

    Productions and mini-plays that address real issues students deal with: academic pressure, peer pressure, cyberbullying, environmental responsibility, and safeguarding.

  5. Student-led learning outcomes

    Towards the end of the year, dedicated members produce an individual output: a production proposal, a 5–7 minute solo theatre piece applying a specific theatre theory, or an original dramatic piece.

This kind of outreach is what sets us apart as a collective rather than just a club.
